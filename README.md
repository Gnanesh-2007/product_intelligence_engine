## 🚀 What It Does

Industrial manufacturers and distributors often maintain product information across fragmented catalogs, inconsistent spreadsheets, technical descriptions, and supplier data.

The **Industrial Product Intelligence Engine** automatically transforms this raw catalog information into a standardized product dataset suitable for downstream commerce, catalog management, validation, and analysis.
